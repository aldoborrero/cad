# ODrive v4-mono, 56 V — independent first review

Date: 2026-09-07. Source checkout: `97fbc08`. Scope: first review before agreeing
the recovery plan. No schematic, PCB, project rules, library, or firmware changes
were made. Findings below come from fresh exports and manufacturer documentation;
the handoff is historical context, not evidence of electrical correctness.

**Verdict: this revision cannot be treated as a functional design awaiting routing.**
There are confirmed errors in the component-to-pad mapping, supply circuit, and
protection logic. Repair and validate those foundations before continuing layout.
Zero geometric DRC errors is reproducible, but does not validate the circuit.

## 1. Measurements and their limits

KiCad 10.0.4, original project basename and adjacent rules, DRC with zone refill
in memory (without saving the board), all severities and schematic parity:

| Check | Fresh result |
|---|---:|
| Footprints | 325 |
| Track segments / vias | 2682 / 454 |
| Unconnected | 204 (DRC and independent pcbnew count agree) |
| Geometric DRC errors | 0 |
| DRC warnings | 318 |
| Schematic parity warnings | 79 |
| ERC errors / warnings | 0 / 68 |

The 318 warnings comprise 199 library footprint differences, 70 dangling tracks,
46 text-height warnings, two dangling vias, and one hole-to-hole warning. They
require classification; these are not 318 independently confirmed defects.

Likewise, **do not interpret the 57 `net_conflict` parity warnings as 57 wrong
connections**. Comparing exported schematic net memberships against board pad
memberships, independently of autogenerated net names, found no differences for
multi-pin nets and no connected board pins absent from the exported netlist.
The other 22 parity warnings concern fields. Several reported missing pins are
explicit NCs or belong to autogenerated nets. The initial suspicion of general
schematic/PCB net divergence was therefore not substantiated. Both representations
can agree while carrying the same wrong component pinout.

## 2. Confirmed blockers

### R01 — All 15 power MOSFETs use an incompatible symbol/pad mapping

Affected: Q1, Q10–Q21, Q50–Q51. The generic `Q_NMOS_GDS` symbol assigns
gate/drain/source to pins 1/2/3. The PCB uses `PowerPAK_SO-8_Single`, whose pads 4
and 5 have **no net** on every one of these devices, including the large drain pad.

For the proposed BSC027N10NS5, physical pins 1–3 are source, pin 4 is gate, and
pins 5–8 are drain. For example, Q10 has its supposed gate on physical source pin
1 and DCBUS on physical source pin 2; its physical gate and drain are unassigned.
Populating the intended device ties nominally different PCB nets through its
common source terminals. This also invalidates the present power-cell placement
and power-path evaluation. [Infineon datasheet, p. 1](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/24/49/infineon-bsc027n10ns5-datasheet-en.pdf?fileId=5546d462689a790c0168c7cb36906441).

### R02 — U3's DRV8353RS custom symbol has the wrong pinout

Examples from exported netlist and board, compared with the 48-pin RS package:

| Physical pin | Present assignment | Manufacturer function |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | CPL | PGND |
| 2 | CPH | VGLS |
| 6 | GHA | VDRAIN |
| 27 | ENABLE | AGND |
| 32 | INLB | nSCS |
| 33 | INHC | ENABLE |
| 40 | nSHDN / GND | DVDD |

The invented nSHDN/NC arrangement also omits real buck and ground functions.
Rebuild the entire symbol against the exact orderable package, including ground
pins, DVDD decoupling and unused-buck handling. [TI DRV835x, pp. 8–9](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv8353.pdf).

### R03 — D2's specified part and assembly polarity disagree

The schematic uses the **bidirectional** `Device:D_TVS` symbol but specifies
**unidirectional** SMDJ58A. Its PCB pad 1, the cathode-marked end of `D_SMC`, is
PGND; pad 2 is DCBUS. Assembly following that marking puts the unidirectional
device into forward conduction on a positive bus. Correct symbol, polarity,
footprint marking and exact MPN together. This is not resolved by a DRC pass.
[Littelfuse SMDJ datasheet, pp. 2 and 5](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/tvs-diodes-smdj-datasheet?assetguid=2d9772b3-822b-4362-914d-b74e0a706638).

### R04 — U50 brake driver has GND and IN+ swapped

The UCC27517A symbol assigns pin 2 to the brake command and pin 3 to PGND.
The manufacturer's DBV package has **2 = GND, 3 = IN+**. The command drives the
device's ground while its non-inverting input is grounded. The hardware brake
cannot be credited with the specified behavior.
[TI UCC27517A, p. 4](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ucc27517a.pdf).

### R05 — U30/U31/U32 load-switch pinouts are wrong

The TPS2553DBV symbols assign IN/OUT to 6/1 and ILIM/FAULT to 4/5. For the
specified SOT-23 DBV package they are **IN=1, OUT=6, ILIM=5, FAULT=4**. Thus the
power direction is reversed and the actual current-limit pin is left NC. The
claimed connector current limits are not implemented by this wiring.
[TI TPS2553, p. 5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2553.pdf).

### R06 — U11's TMUX1204 custom symbol is also misnumbered

The physical DGS package has GND=3, VDD=6, A0=1, A1=10, D=8, EN=5.
The exported circuit instead puts GPIO_6 on physical ground, AVCC on A1,
USB_VBUS_SENSE on D, and AGND on EN. Its analog channel map cannot work as
documented. [TI TMUX1204, p. 3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmux1204.pdf).

### R07 — U25 asserts nFAULT when both rails are good

U27 ANDs the active-high 12 V and 5 V power-good signals. Its output drives a
74LVC1G06 open-drain **inverter**. A high input pulls nFAULT low; a low input
releases it. This is the opposite of the intended rail-fault behavior. Correct
the truth table, including startup and brownout, rather than changing firmware
to ignore nFAULT. [TI SN74LVC1G06, function table](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g06.pdf).

### R08 — C113 is outside the LM5164 bootstrap-capacitor specification

C113 is 100 nF / 25 V between BST and SW. The LM5164 specifies **2.2 nF**, with
1.5–2.5 nF in its absolute-maximum table, and specifies a 50 V X7R part in the
pin instructions. This is a different bootstrap circuit from the LMR51430,
which does specify 100 nF. Pin numbering for both buck ICs was checked and agrees
with their datasheets; their circuits still require full component-value review.
[TI LM5164, pp. 3–4](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm5164.pdf),
[TI LMR51430, p. 3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lmr51430.pdf).

## 3. Additional 56 V design gaps

**R09 — OV threshold calculation does not match the claimed 60 V trip.** Using
the documented 56 V selections R70=18 kΩ, R71=1 kΩ, R93=15.4 kΩ, R94=10 kΩ,
R95=130 kΩ, reference=1.24 V, and ideal output levels 0/3.3 V:

```text
Vbus,rise = 19 × 1.24 × (1 + 15.4/10 + 15.4/130) = 62.63 V
Vbus,fall = Vbus,rise − 19 × 3.3 × 15.4/130 = 55.21 V
```

These are circuit calculations, not measured thresholds. The rising threshold
requires 3.2965 V from a buffer powered at nominal 3.3 V. Offset, resistor/reference
tolerance, output swing and supply variation have not been budgeted. Therefore
the intended 60 V backstop is not established, particularly beside 63 V bulk caps.
The 150 Ω onboard brake absorbs only 24 W at 60 V; adequacy for the actual motor's
regeneration is unproven without its energy/power requirements and supply behavior.

**R10 — VM is not a 100 V supply input.** Even after correcting R02, the design
intends to tie VM to DCBUS. VM has a 75 V operating / 80 V absolute maximum;
VDRAIN has different limits. A TVS maximum clamp of 93.6 V does not protect VM to
its rating. Separate supply/limiting and transient coordination must be designed;
the family marketing voltage is not a pin rating.
[TI DRV835x, pp. 10–11](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv8353.pdf).

**R11 — There is no consistent, assembly-ready mono 56 V BOM.** The motor-cell
export still says NVMFS5C628NL and 1 mΩ shunts R22–R24, without 56 V MPN fields.
`bom-variants.md` instead selects BSC060N10NS3G and WSLP2512L5000FEA, while
`v4-design.md` selects BSC027N10NS5 and CSS2H-2512R-L500F. The BOM document still
contains dual-axis references/counts and proposes SMD electrolytics for a board
with radial through-hole footprints. Each substitution needs its own land-pattern,
loss, gate-drive and current-scaling validation. Existing cost totals are not a
quote for this mono board.

**R12 — Firmware compatibility is an unimplemented work item.** No DRV8353 driver
implementation was found in the supplied firmware checkout. `firmware-port.md`
also describes three status registers through 0x02, whereas `v4-design.md` assigns
0x02 to control, and the documents disagree on shunt values/gains. Register map,
mono operation, fault recovery, PWM/ADC mapping, auxiliary mux, GPIO restrictions
and 56 V limits need an explicit buildable firmware target. No firmware build,
motor test or hardware validation was performed in this review.

## 4. Layout and verification observations

Fresh front-copper and schematic renders were inspected. The front render makes
the unrouted large MOSFET pads visible; logic is densely packed in the lower half.
The power-input sheet has overlapping capacitor values and text outside the page.
Electrical correctness and schematic readability both need acceptance criteria.

Do not preserve the power-cell geometry just because its placement score passes:
the electrical pads used to judge its gate/commutation paths were wrong (R01/R02).
The phase ampacity, Kelvin topology, return planes, USB ESD placement, creepage,
heatsink and thermal paths remain unvalidated. No current rating is endorsed here.

The existing `power_eval.py` counts named zones and vias, excludes tracks narrower
than 0.5 mm from its phase-width test, and omits several supply nets. Those are
limited heuristics, not proof of connected, adequately sized current paths. It
does not establish correspondence to the purchased device's physical pins.

## 6. Evidence and reproduction

Scratch evidence: `.scratch/v4-56v-audit/` contains `mono.xml`, `erc.json`,
`drc.json`, `board.json`, source SHA-256 hashes, the read-only inspection/MCP probe
scripts, tool catalogs, and SVG/PNG renders (`front.png`, `power-input.png`,
`motorcell.png`, `rails.png`, `brake.png`). These are regenerable local artifacts.

From the repo root in the existing KiCad environment:

```bash
mkdir -p .scratch/v4-56v-audit
kicad-cli sch export netlist --format kicadxml \
  -o .scratch/v4-56v-audit/mono.xml \
  projects/odrive/kicad/odrive-v4-mono/odrive-v4-mono.kicad_sch
kicad-cli sch erc --format json -o .scratch/v4-56v-audit/erc.json \
  projects/odrive/kicad/odrive-v4-mono/odrive-v4-mono.kicad_sch
kicad-cli pcb drc --refill-zones --schematic-parity --severity-all --format json \
  -o .scratch/v4-56v-audit/drc.json \
  projects/odrive/kicad/odrive-v4-mono/odrive-v4-mono.kicad_pcb
```

This is a first review, not a complete component audit or fabrication release.
It is sufficient to establish the recovery plan's first dependency: a validated
56 V circuit, exact MPN/package/pad mappings and firmware contract, followed by
an informed decision on layout reuse. Motor, encoder, current/torque target,
regeneration energy and supply behavior remain inputs to that plan.
